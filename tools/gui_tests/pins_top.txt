# Pins tab on a top-level design (the synthesized 008-counter with its CCF): the chip pins before packing (the IO
# buffers, not placed yet), after packing (each on the pad of the CCF, with the cells of its IO and their settings)
# and after placing. Implementing the design as a partition (Partitions tab) lists partition ports instead. A pin
# activated in the list (double click) selects its IO cell, a cell row that cell.
# design: counter_synth
resize 1400 1000
tab Pins
assert_label pinsSummary Chip pins
assert_label pinsSummary 10 pins, 2 inputs, 8 outputs; 0 placed
dock pinList
check Implement this design as a partition on
assert_label pinsSummary Partition ports
check Implement this design as a partition off
assert_label pinsSummary Chip pins
action Pack
waitidle
assert_label pinsSummary 10 pins, 2 inputs, 8 outputs; 10 placed
dock pinList
# the pads of the CCF
dock pinList IO_SB_A8
assert_status CELL $iopadmap$top.clk
dock pinList IO_EA_A7
assert_status CELL $iopadmap$top.led_7
# the IO's settings (GateMate: on the IOSEL in the pad's tile)
dock pinList $iopadmap$top.clk$iosel
assert_status CELL $iopadmap$top.clk$iosel
shot 01_packed.png
action Place
waitidle
assert_label pinsSummary 10 placed
dock pinList led[7]
assert_status CELL $iopadmap$top.led_7
shot 02_placed.png
