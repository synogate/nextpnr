# Pins page (beside Cells and Nets) on a top-level design (the synthesized 008-counter with its CCF): the chip
# pins before packing (the IO buffers, not placed yet), after packing (each on the pad of the CCF, with the cells of its IO and their settings)
# and after placing. Implementing the design as a partition (Partitions tab) lists partition ports instead. A pin
# activated in the list (double click) is zoomed to in the device view.
# design: counter_synth
resize 1400 1000
tab Pins
assert_label pinsSummary 10 chip pins
dock pinList Chip pins
dock pinList
check Implement this design as a partition on
assert_label pinsSummary 10 ports to the parent design
check Implement this design as a partition off
assert_label pinsSummary 10 chip pins
action Pack
waitidle
assert_label pinsSummary 10 chip pins
dock pinList
# the pads of the CCF
dock pinList IO_SB_A8
dock pinList IO_EA_A7
# the IO's settings (GateMate: on the IOSEL in the pad's tile)
dock pinList $iopadmap$top.clk$iosel

waitidle
